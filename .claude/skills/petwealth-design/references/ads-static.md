# Paid static ads (Meta, also ChatGPT and partner placements)

Figma home: Paid Ads file `jzr4cIp4dKWHkT4AlvugXg`. Each campaign gets its own page (named `MMDDYY <Campaign>`), organized as ACTIVE / ARCHIVE / IMAGE ASSETS sections. Keep that structure. A Top Ads board in the same file ranks statics by purchases: borrow from winners.

**File conventions (from the latest pages):**
- Top-level ALL-CAPS sections per campaign (BLACK FRIDAY / HOLIDAY / RETARGETING), dated sub-sections ("… (Nov 20)"), and explicit status words: "PARKED · DO NOT EXPORT", "DEPRIORITIZED", "NOT FOR EXPORT", "Motion graphic assets (not ads)".
- Frame names: `<Concept code> · <Concept> · <Product> · <Cat|Dog> · <4:5|9:16> · <variant>` (codes like R1, A4b, B11d).
- Inspo sits on the left of each row, captioned "INSPIRATION · Brand (who, date)", then 4:5 / 9:16 pairs. A grey caption (about 30px bold) above each frame and a short note under it.
- Each section carries a "⚠ BEFORE EXPORTING 9:16: delete the MOCK…" note.
- Top Ads board rows: "#N Cat · Fecal · Concept · Version" with the 4:5, 9:16 and a metrics card naming the export file.

## Photography

Candid, UGC-style photos perform best in ads: phone-shot, real homes, natural light, the pet as the hero. This is the one channel where UGC leads; email and the website stay a little more editorial.

## Formats

- Every concept ships as **4:5 (1080x1350)** and **9:16 (1080x1920)**. Add 1:1 only if asked.
- Each concept typically has cat and dog versions. Every ad uses a different animal and different imagery.

## 9:16 Story safe zones

- Top ~250px: profile row ("petwealth · Sponsored"). Keep headlines and badges below it.
- Bottom: Meta's "Shop now" CTA. Keep all text, logos, offers and product clear of y ≈ 1600 to 1812. Background bands may run behind it.
- Recent pages carry two mock layers inside each 9:16 frame: `MOCK · Story top bar (hide before export)` (y 0 to 200) and `MOCK · Shop now (Story CTA) · DELETE BEFORE EXPORT` (y 1680 to 1920, pill at y 1728). Older pages use a "STORY SAFE-ZONE MOCK" frame. **Hide or delete these before every export**, and say you did.
- **Check that nothing real is nested inside a mock layer** before deleting it (a logo was found parented to a Shop now mock). Move it out first.
- Approved Black Friday 9:16s run content from about y 216 to y 1660. Treat y 1600 as the target and y 1680 (where the mock starts) as the hard limit.
- Use the space: on 9:16 make text, checkmarks and product bigger and fill down to the safe line. Don't shrink to fit.

## Versioning 4:5 to 9:16

- If the 4:5 photo was cropped from a taller original, put the full original back and move the overlays by the crop offset. Never zoom-fill the 4:5 image.
- Plain studio or gradient backgrounds: stretch the empty band above and below the product. Never leave a visible 4:5 rectangle or seam.
- Never distort images (watch for unequal x/y scale in crops).
- Spread the copy over the height (headline high, offer lower) instead of centering the 4:5 block.
- When rebuilding a concept for another product (e.g. fecal to respiratory), clone the original frames and swap content only; match size and position exactly.
- Page layout: inspo at left, then 4:5 / 9:16 pairs 80px apart, rows evenly spaced.

## Layouts that work

- **Checkmark list:** big green filled circles with white ticks, one benefit per line, e.g. "One litter box scoop, collected at home" / "Free return shipping" / "Results in 24–48 hrs.*" / "Screens for Giardia, Tritrichomonas, Coccidia + 24 more pathogens".
- **Bottom "screens for" bar:** full-width navy bar, one line ("Screens for 27 pathogens, including Giardia, Tritrichomonas & Coccidia"), fine print below.
- **Quote-first testimonial:** stars, big verbatim quote, attribution, then checkmarks and kit. Works over a full-bleed pet photo with a dark gradient.
- **iPhone Notes card** over a candid photo; checklist items must match what's visible in the photo.
- **Program callout:** navy two-line badge over photos; on light or tan backgrounds use a soft eyebrow (colored rule + caps label, "Special pricing available" below). Boxed badges on tan look bulky.
- **Halo / circle product layout:** product perfectly centered in the circle with the box/tray gap on the circle's center; respiratory uses two callouts only (VIRUSES left, BACTERIA right), each group's dot sitting on the circle edge.
- **App result screens for ads:** simplified version: pet photo on top, enlarged health-status card, stats row, nothing below.
- **Kit-color product card** (a top Black Friday performer, shipped for every kit, cat and dog): full background in the kit color (forest green, teal, purple, violet, navy; slate for the Complete Kit) with a soft oval glow behind the centered open box + tube. Stack from top: bold headline (about 88px, caps, tight tracking) near y≈96, bold subhead (about 38px), one-line body (about 32px), product in the middle band, a short lead-in line, the offer in the lightened kit accent (about 105px), fine print (about 26px), white logo bottom center (about 250 wide). An "on photo" variant runs the same stack over a real product photo fading into the kit color.
- **Retargeting set:** offer bar on top + real customer photo + serif quote pill + white review card; POV text in monospace over a darkened customer photo with lilac highlight blocks; review card + handwritten arrow note; full Okendo review screenshot; "BUY 2 KITS / SAVE 20%" big-type. Review cards mimic Okendo ("Verified Buyer · Cat Respiratory Kit", green check, gold stars).
- **Native UI mimics:** iPhone Notes lists, iMessage threads, TikTok caption boxes (white rounded boxes, bold black text) over real photos.
- **Editorial serif note:** short serif statement on black or stone ("(we never do this) our only sale of the year") with lilac checkmarks.
- Also recurring: yellow sticky notes on real box photos, big single number ("28") with a curved lilac ribbon of pathogen groups, the line-art "Loop" ring, long-copy headline over a photo.
- **Top Ads winners to borrow from:** lab printout list (monospace on paper), illustrated pet with an italic serif quote card on lilac, hand-hold with a column of pathogen pills plus a yellow "28 TARGETS, ONE SAMPLE" bar, iPhone Notes list, big number + ribbon, Loop ring, kit-color product card.

## Ad typography and color (beyond the brand core)

- Statics use Gelica / Google Sans Flex (fecal and respiratory sets) and Creolia (Loop, carousels). Holiday ads used Instrument Serif headlines. Mimic fonts are allowed only inside the thing they mimic: Inter or SF Pro in iOS UI, IBM Plex Mono for printout/POV text, Caveat for handwriting tags.
- Offer lines are often caps with middot separators: "OUR ONLY SALE OF THE YEAR · 20% OFF ORDERS $150+".
- **Performance and seasonal colors:** paid ads may go louder than the brand core when a campaign calls for it: saturated red for Black Friday and holiday sets (full-bleed backgrounds, footer bars, price ladders with VALUE / REGULAR / SALE labels and a "LOWEST PRICE OF THE YEAR" bar), burnt orange for Complete Kit / upsell moments, yellow bars for offers and target counts. Keep these to sale and performance work; brand, organic, email and web stay in the core palette.

## Recurring ad components

- Offer pill (about 573x70, centered): "20% off orders $150+ · Ends 11/30". Offer bar: full width, about 92px, at the very top. Footer line: "Our only sale of the year · Ends 11/30".
- Logo: white or dark, about 180 to 250 wide, bottom center (or top center on photo ads).
- Review card: white, about 24px radius, name, verified line, gold stars, title, body. Quote pill: white with a serif quote in curly quotes.
- Sale stickers: the lilac promo seal, or a navy starburst ("20% OFF / BLACK FRIDAY").

## Copy on ads

- Follow `copy-and-claims.md`. Short, specific, and the 24 to 48 hour claim always gets its asterisk.
- Sale wording can mix "our only sale of the year", "Black Friday" and "biggest sale of the year" (see `copy-and-claims.md`).
- No program prices. No condition names on the image. "Most comprehensive" is approved.
- Keep CTAs mostly in the ad's primary text and the platform button; on-image CTAs only if the concept calls for them.

## Inspiration

- Atria indexes ads only (use it for ad inspo, not emails). Put inspo frames beside our versions in Figma with the creative direction written underneath.
- Recreate the idea and structure, never copy another brand's assets or wording.

## Export

- Per-frame export at exactly 1x (frame's long edge = output long edge).
- JPEG quality 95, 4:4:4 (no chroma subsampling), flattened on white.
- Names: `Petwealth_<Product|Audience>_<Cat|Dog>_<NN-Concept>_<Vx-Desc>_<4x5|9x16>.jpg`.
- Drive: `Marketing/Paid Media/Ad Creative/MM DD YY/<Cat|Dog>/...`. This folder holds **final, launch-ready Meta ads only**. Fixes to live ads go in a `Replacements/` subfolder with identical filenames. Working assets go in `Marketing/Photography/UGC/`.
- Export only after approval. Retargeting sets live in their own group and folder, separate from prospecting.
