# Review checklist (run before showing or exporting anything)

Screenshot every frame or page you touched and go through this list. Fix what fails before sharing.

## Copy
- [ ] No em dashes or en-dash punctuation anywhere (headlines, body, captions, alt text, file names).
- [ ] Tests named fecal / oral / respiratory, never body areas.
- [ ] Counts match `copy-and-claims.md` and the correct species (cat fecal 27, dog fecal 28, cat resp 6, dog resp 11, oral 4, cat Complete 37, dog Complete 42).
- [ ] Prices match the live site and the current offer, and are consistent across every surface in the campaign.
- [ ] "Results in 24 to 48 hours" has its asterisk and fine print.
- [ ] Reviews verbatim, matching species and product, real reviewer.
- [ ] No program prices in ads, no diagnosis language, no condition names on ad images.
- [ ] Pathogen names spelled correctly (feline herpesvirus spelled out, not "FHV-1").

## Imagery
- [ ] Packaging is the real packshot or real footage, not AI-drawn. Box text legible and correct.
- [ ] Two-piece product images have the gap at the exact horizontal center.
- [ ] Kit colors correct for species (cat fecal green, dog fecal teal, cat resp deep purple, dog resp violet, oral navy).
- [ ] Zoom in: no extra fingers or thumbs, no merged animals, no human teeth on pets, no stray brand logos or lettering.
- [ ] No halos around cutouts on dark backgrounds; contact shadows look natural.
- [ ] Nothing composite-moved inside a real product photo; nothing stretched or squished.

## Layout
- [ ] Matches the reference or original design side by side at the same size (desktop and mobile for web/email).
- [ ] Alignment matches the source (left vs centered), spacing even, nothing cut off.
- [ ] 9:16: content clear of the top ~250px and the Shop now zone (y ≈ 1600 to 1812); space used, not shrunk.
- [ ] Email/web renders at 320, 375 and desktop widths without overflow.
- [ ] Fonts actually loaded (not a fallback), Creolia/Plus Jakarta Sans or the file's established ad fonts.

## Housekeeping
- [ ] New frames in clear empty space; nothing overlapping; temporary placeholders deleted.
- [ ] Nobody's work deleted or overwritten; superseded versions archived.
- [ ] Mock layers (Shop now mocks, safe-zone overlays) hidden before export, and restored if still wanted.
- [ ] Exports at 1x, correct format and naming, saved to the right folder (Ad Creative = final Meta ads only).
- [ ] Nothing published, sent or scheduled without the owner's OK.

## Brand feel
- [ ] Passes the Petwealth filter: recognizable as Petwealth with the logo removed.
- [ ] Feels intelligent, calm, premium, compassionate, preventive, trusted. Nothing loud, gimmicky, salesy, fear-based or cutesy.
- [ ] Pet is the focal point; one display moment; headlines tracked tight; lilac used sparingly.
- [ ] It has gone to Anya for sign-off before export, launch, send or going live (she approves everything).

## The report
- [ ] Lead with what changed, include the screenshot or link, end with the one question that needs an answer.
