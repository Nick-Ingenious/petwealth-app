## Slides

- **Grid:** 120px left margin; eyebrow at y≈120; Creolia headline at y≈166 (nudged about 6px left for optical alignment); takeaway or footnote line near y≈955; footer at y≈1040 with the deck or event name bottom-left and the page number right-aligned.
- **Footer text by audience:** "Private & Confidential 2026" on partner and wholesale decks; the event name on event decks ("Kennel Connection User Group · Hershey 2026"); the deck title on internal decks.
- **Dark house style:** Slate `#19242B` ground, `#222F37` cards (radius 24, no stroke), white titles, `#B8C2C9` body at 150% line height. Headlines Creolia about 80px (titles up to about 110px), body about 28 to 32px, eyebrows Plus Jakarta Sans caps about 22px with wide tracking, lilac on dark and teal on light.
- **Light style:** Stone `#F8F2EA` grounds with white cards; some partner product slides use a cool pale lavender-grey ground (about `#F4F5FD`) with lavender cards.
- **Key phrase highlight:** in current pieces a solid lilac word on dark ("only sale", "Made simple") or teal on light, rather than a gradient. Test names can sit in the kit color ("Dog **fecal** health kit").
- **Image-led slide:** photo on the right two-thirds fading into slate on the left.
- **Data / results slides:** eyebrow "DAY 1 · MON 11/16", headline with the verdict in green; four KPI cards with a status-colored stroke (caps label, Creolia number, delta line, baseline line); a table with the goal column in lilac and actuals as status pills (good `#3DD68C`, below goal `#FF6B6B`); a takeaway paragraph at the bottom.
- **Timeline:** horizontal line with lilac dots, bold date, phase in lilac, bullets under.
- **Partner-app proposal slides:** real partner screenshots in phone mockups with native editable overlays; an outlined "TODAY IN [PARTNER]" pill beside a lilac "PROPOSED · …" pill; teal tag for "uses existing screens" vs lilac for "adds something new". Use the partner's own feature names. No generic "IDEA 1" eyebrows.
- Structure for partner pitches: title, overview, the idea (one per slide), how it works, proof, offer, rollout / next steps. Show prices only if signed or public; otherwise "member pricing". No filler copy.
- Final deck in one clean row; superseded drafts go in an Archive section. Frames named "NN · Title", image layers "IMG · …".

## Recurring components

- **Test cards:** packshot, test name in Creolia in the kit color, "For when you see…", caps "SCREENS FOR 28 PATHOGENS, INCLUDING:", white pathogen pills with an outlined "+ 24 more" pill (lead pathogen pill filled in the kit color).
- **Steps:** numbered circles on a connector line (horizontal or vertical), bold title + one sentence. Canonical four: Register your kit (scan the QR) / Collect at home / Send to the lab (prepaid) / Results in 24–48 hours.
- **Section nav tabs:** row of circle icons with labels, active one lilac.
- **Dark checklists:** lilac filled circles with slate ticks.
- **QR block (print):** QR code, "Scan to learn more about our wholesale program", "Or email …".
- **Stat row:** Creolia figure over a caps label ("24–48h TURNAROUND", "3 kits", "0 vet trips").
- **Feature trio:** icon + "Designed with vets, trusted by vets / Clear answers you can act on / Results in 24 to 48 hours".
- **Wholesale product slide:** title, italic subtitle, "CONSIDER TESTING **PROACTIVELY** OR WHEN YOU NOTICE" (key word lilac), outlined symptom chips, TESTS FOR copy, MSRP in Creolia.
- **Pathogen names:** italicize genus and species only ("*Clostridium perfringens* alpha toxin"); "spp." stays roman.

## By audience

- **Vets:** frame the vet's role ("Recommend & share", "Review together"); "Why vets rely on Petwealth" (in-house lab, clear results, 24 to 48 hours); full pathogen detail; a results or lab-report preview.
- **Boarding, kennels, daycares:** "Why facilities partner with us": screen new guests before booking, a revenue line, easy for any staff, reopen sooner. Collection is on-site. A founder story slide works well at events.
- **Wholesale and retail:** MSRP, a QR to wholesale info, the On-Site vs At-Home kit split ("For your team" / "For your clients"), "Private & Confidential".
- **Consumers (print):** "Your pet can't tell you what's wrong. Testing can." with a note that it doesn't replace vet care.

## Google Docs (briefs, plans, talking points)

- Plus Jakarta Sans throughout (title, headings, body, tables). Bold headings.
- Visible spacing between sections and around lists, set through paragraph spacing (headings about 24pt above / 8pt below and keep-with-next; body 10pt below; space before and after lists and after tables). Don't use empty paragraphs for spacing, they turn into blank bullets next to lists.
- Edit existing Docs in place rather than recreating them.
- No em dashes.

## Google Sheets (non-financial trackers)

- Clean header band, frozen header row, consistent column widths, inputs clearly marked. For financial models use the system in `dashboards-and-data.md`.
- Deliver spreadsheets as live Google Sheets, not .xlsx attachments.
