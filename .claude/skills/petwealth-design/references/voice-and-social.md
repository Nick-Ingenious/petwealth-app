# Voice, audience and the social media playbook

Where the playbook's sample copy conflicts with current product facts (it uses placeholder numbers like "12 pathogens", "35+ pathogens" and "72-hour results"), use `copy-and-claims.md`. ## Who we are, in one line

"Love, backed by science." A health intelligence platform, not a pet brand. Think Oura, Function Health, Apple Health, Seed. Never big-box pet retail (Chewy, PetSmart, Petco).

## Who we speak to

**Pet parents:** thoughtful, modern, design-conscious, wellness-oriented. They value preventative care, want clarity not noise, invest in premium trusted solutions, see pets as companions. They aren't looking for more products, they're looking for better decisions. "We are speaking to those who expect more, and recognize it when they see it."

**Pet professionals** (vets and telehealth, animal health partners, breeders, premium resorts and daycares): they want better systems, measurable outcomes, structured diagnostic-grade data, scalable workflows, and a differentiated offering for their clients. They want partners, not vendors. "We are infrastructure for modern pet care."

## Personality

Emotionally intelligent. Quietly confident. Scientifically grounded. Every piece should feel: **intelligent, calm, premium, compassionate, preventive, trusted.**

| Trait | Means | Ask yourself |
|---|---|---|
| Emotional intelligence | Lead with the bond; make science personal | Does this strengthen the bond between pets and their people? |
| Quiet confidence | Authoritative without being loud; science supports the story, never becomes it | Would a vet be comfortable with this message? |
| Human and clear | Complex ideas, simply; every sentence reads like conversation | Does this help pet parents understand their pet? |
| Premium simplicity | Luxury is clarity; every word earns its place | Would Oura or Apple Health publish this? |

## Voice

Speak like a trusted guide, never a salesperson. **Lead with emotion, support with science. Benefits before features. Outcomes, never technology. Empowering, never intimidating.** Plain language, about an 8th-grade reading level, one thought per sentence.

**Use:** "Trusted by vets." "Personalized health insights." "Answers you can act on." "Protect wellbeing before problems start." "Your pet's story." "Happier, healthier years together." "Prevention is priceless." "Pet longevity is the new luxury."

**Avoid:** disease detection or diagnosis claims, jargon ("DNA report", "algorithm", "multiplex qPCR"), fear-based or guilt messaging, puns ("Paw-fect", "Happy tails guaranteed"), overpromising, generic buzzwords, anything a vet wouldn't comfortably share.

**In practice**
- Instead of "Bordetella bronchiseptica detected." say "We found bacteria commonly associated with kennel cough."
- Instead of "Our proprietary AI algorithm analyzes hundreds of biomarkers…" say "We translate complex biology into simple next steps."
- Instead of "99.9% accurate multiplex qPCR panel." say "The same testing standard your vet trusts, from home."

**Tone guardrails (the slide toward bad)**
- Lifestyle should feel *aspirational*, never *transactional* (no hard selling, no "buy now" energy, no cluttered product pushes).
- Emotion should feel *intentional*, never *reactive* (no forced sentiment, guilt, drama or "cute overload").
- Information should feel *conversational*, never *clinical* (no data dumps, sterile lab language, lectures).

## Messaging pillars (every post supports at least one)

1. **Love, backed by science:** emotion first, science gives confidence (owner stories, the bond, lifestyle).
2. **Preventive health:** stay ahead, not react (education, prevention, daily habits).
3. **Actionable insights:** science into everyday decisions (reports, results, how-to).
4. **Trust and credibility:** confidence through science (vet partnerships, lab, validation).
5. **Better everyday living:** show the impact, not just the data (wellness, nutrition, longevity).

## Guardrails (why we're careful)

Stay scientifically accurate, never overstate. Protect our IP: share the value of the results framework, not the blueprint. One voice across website, ads, emails, social and reports. Diagnostic claims invite regulatory and platform (health-ad policy) scrutiny, overpromising creates complaints. **When a claim or stat feels uncertain, review it internally before it goes live.**

## Social grid (Instagram is the primary brand surface)

- **Checkerboard the grounds:** never two dark posts touching in any direction. Dark tiles are punctuation.
- **Anchor every row with an editorial photo.** Type-only rows read like a brochure.
- **Reels move diagonally** across the grid, about one per row.
- **The 15-post mix:** 5 photo-led, 5 educational, 3 product and data, 2 owner stories. Macro texture shots reset the eye.
- **Series get a badge** ("Petwealth 101", "Decoded", "Vet Notes").
- Positioning between two references: Maev's warm editorial photography on Oura's disciplined dark canvas, with series and data-as-content. "Photography for the heart, reports for the proof."

**Weekly cadence:** Mon big statement · Tue symptom education · Wed lifestyle · Thu carousel · Fri report UI or myth vs fact · Sat product lifestyle (the only overtly commercial day) · Sun quiet, before-symptoms. Reels Tue / Thu / Sat. Never three educational posts in a row.

## Organic templates

Big Statement (4:5), Symptom Education carousel cover (4:5), Myth vs Fact (1:1), Report UI on a glass card (4:5), Saveable Checklist (4:5), Owner Story (4:5, photo-led). Dashed frames in the templates mark photo swap zones; everything else is locked.

**Carousel arc (4 to 9 slides, one idea each, every slide readable alone):** Hook (serif claim on photo) → Recognition (scattered type) → Value (numbered tips) → Proof (diagram on blurred photo) → Stat (flat ground) → Bridge (soft CTA + chips). Alternate photo and flat grounds; end on a soft CTA; the cover must work as a standalone grid post.

## Overlay rules

- **Type lives in thirds:** headlines top third, body bottom third. The middle belongs to the subject; never cover eyes or faces.
- **Scrim, never boxes:** bottom scrim from transparent at 45% to 88% slate. No text boxes, no full-frame washes above 35%.
- **One type moment per slide:** serif display or scattered type, never both. Body copy always sans, white at 85 to 90% opacity.
- **Scattered type:** six words max, follows the subject's contour, reads top to bottom and left to right, italic only on the pivot word, once per carousel.
- **Blur is a focus tool** only when a diagram or data must lead.
- **Consistent furniture:** lockup top-left, arrow bottom-right, dots bottom-center, same on every slide.
- **Four grounds, rotated:** editorial photo (with scrim), stone flat, dark gradient (`#19242B → #143E4A`), macro texture. Never two flats or two darks in a row.

## Paid social (playbook view)

- Headlines under 40 characters. One data device per frame. Reassurance-led, never fear-based. Every ad answers one objection; primary text leads with the symptom or the proof, never the technology.
- **Funnel:** Prospecting (symptom-led product, breed reels; feed 4:5 + Reels) → Consideration (vet endorsement, how-it-works carousel; 4:5 + 1:1) → Retargeting (testimonials, Story end card + offer; 9:16). Species-split targeting. Refresh creative every 4 to 6 weeks.
- Specs: 4:5 1080x1350 (primary), 1:1 1080x1080, 9:16 1080x1920 with copy inside the center 1080x1420. Carousels upload in order. See `ads-static.md` for the house build and export rules, which take precedence for Meta exports.

## Community management

Lead with empathy, build conversations, protect trust, be timely. Comments: answer clearly, celebrate wins, thank people. DMs: warm, helpful, escalate support. **Medical questions: never diagnose, recommend a vet, share education.** Negative feedback: stay calm, acknowledge, move to private message. Before replying: helpful, kind, accurate, on brand, builds trust?

## Workflow and quality control

Brainstorm → draft copy → design → internal review → compliance check → schedule → publish → analyze. Final files live in shared Google Drive folders, never only on someone's computer. Publish from brand accounts with current logos and templates.

**Six checkpoints before anything ships:** brand voice → scientific review → copy review → design review → final check → approval.

**The Petwealth filter:** if the logo were removed, would someone still recognize this as Petwealth? If not, revisit it before publishing.
