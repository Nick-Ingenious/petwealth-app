# Video ads

Finished videos live in `Marketing/Paid Media/Ad Creative/<Month YYYY>/<MM DD YY>/Video(s)/<Cat|Dog>/`. The all-time winner is "Cat Fecal One Scoop Real Answers" (`August 2026/8 9 26/Cat Fecal UGC Video Ads/`).

## What works

- **The proven formula:** dread → one easy action → relief → one next step. Winners run 10 to 13 seconds; evergreen ads 14 to 30; founder videos 30 to 60.
- **Beat order:** symptom or named pet → the kit (unbox, scoop or swab, tube in frame) → mailer → app result scroll → next step (the app's lilac Ask a Vet card, or "took it to our vet"). Include a rule-out beat in the app arc (e.g. "Parvo + Salmonella clear").
- **Hook in the first 1 to 2 seconds as on-screen text over the action.** No logo intro. Naming "this scoop" / "this at-home test" in the hook tested as the strongest lever.
- **Hook formats that work:** first-person symptom line (the winner); "3 reasons I would NOT buy this" reverse psychology ("0/10, would buy again"); problem / solution split screen; "how I used to handle it vs how I get answers now"; "4 easy steps"; comment-reply bubble; street or expo question; Slack or iMessage chat mimic; infomercial ("BUT WAIT, there's more!"); day rewind. Social Q&A works when the question contains a belief or objection ("his teeth look totally fine, why would I pay for this").
- **Endings are conclusive:** what the test found and what happened next.
- **Footage:** real phone UGC with a named pet dominates. The app result screen recording appears in almost every ad. Also used: founder talking head (booth backdrop), green-screen founder over B-roll, overhead paper signs, kit drops, website or cart screen recordings, citation screenshots, black-and-white flashback inserts. The kit appears as the box in a hand, the open tube with scoop, the lilac bubble mailer, and the Complete Kit lid reveal.
- Every video ad clicks through to the matching product page.

## Sale versions: retrofit a winner

The fastest sale video is a proven winner with: the original hook plus a second line ("and it's 20% off"), an offer ribbon or ticker during the video, and a 2 to 4 second end card (small pill headline, big serif "20% off", disclaimer line, logo, on dark slate, cream or a blurred photo). Evergreen winners carry no logo or end card; they end on the app's next-step card.

## Formats and delivery

- 9:16 (1080x1920 or 4K 2160x3840). Sale videos are 9:16 only. Keep captions and overlays inside the Story safe zones from `ads-static.md`.
- Deliver one final file per ad into the dated Videos folder. When hooks are being tested, each hook version is its own named final file; drafts and earlier rounds stay in working folders.
- Export at full resolution (check that nothing ships at 540x960).

## On-screen text

Pick one caption style per video:
1. **Plain white sans with a thin shadow** (the winner's style): short phrases, hook in the upper third.
2. **TikTok boxes:** white rounded box with black text; emphasis lines in a lilac box. The most common style in recent evergreen ads.
3. **Black box, white text:** good for before/after and testimonial lines.
4. **Word-by-word karaoke captions** with the current or key word in lilac: founder and talking-head videos.
5. **Dark translucent box with a serif:** infomercial and step-by-step formats.

Recurring overlays:
- **Persistent top title:** a small two-line white-box headline that stays on screen the whole video ("how I'm doing Black Friday as a cat mom"), with captions rolling underneath.
- **Offer lockups during sales:** white box "THE ONLY SALE OF THE YEAR" with a tilted lilac "20% OFF" pill; a lilac scrolling ticker ("BLACK FRIDAY SALE • 20% OFF • THE ONLY SALE OF THE YEAR"); a tilted lilac ribbon; a lilac starburst ("Save $158"); a dark disclaimer pill ("On orders $150+. Free shipping. Ends 11/30.") kept above the Shop now zone.
- **Footnote:** any "24-48 hours" claim carries "*From the time our lab receives your sample."
- **Graphics:** pathogen chips ("+8 more"), checklist strike-throughs, picture-in-picture app cards.
- One headline per video. Sale wording can mix "our only sale of the year", "Black Friday" and "biggest sale"; in native UGC-style overlays drop "our" so it doesn't read as the brand talking.
- Lilac is the accent across captions, pills, ribbons and tickers. End cards use a serif display face; captions use a TikTok-style sans.

## Audio

- Native sound plus first-person voiceover (real or AI voice). Generated R&B or lo-fi music; foley (thuds, paper swish) on product moments.
- **Voiceover at 1.0x by default.** Don't speed voices up unless Anya asks for it.
- Commercial-safe generated or licensed music only. Never trending songs in paid ads.

## Editing rules

- **Never replace or redraw packaging.** For a blurry product freeze-frame, use a genuinely sharp nearby frame of the same take, translated into place (and place the freeze source a few ms past the frame time). Deblur, AI upscales and packshot swaps were rejected.
- Match clips with per-clip gain; don't apply blanket exposure lifts. Tone-map iPhone HDR clips rather than lifting them.
- When physical signs carry the headline, put captions and subjects at the bottom.
- Use AI video only for inserts (adding a pet, an effect) on top of real footage, never for the product. Similes become literal shapes: describe physics and texture and list banned shapes. Added objects drift off screen unless told to stay fixed.

## QC before delivery (most past errors were here)

- Sample stills across the whole timeline and check: warped packaging, extra limbs, text glitches, seams.
- **Counts match the species:** cat fecal 27, dog fecal 28 (cat and dog versions of the same ad have shipped with the counts swapped). App screens show current counts, not old "of 11" screens.
- **App mock copy is correct:** pet names match the footage, viral findings aren't called bacteria, turnaround reads "24 to 48 hours".
- Sample method matches the kit (respiratory is a nasal swab, with an optional eye swab; not "cheek swab" only).
- File names match the content (species and test).
- Claims follow `copy-and-claims.md`; soften VO claims on screen if needed ("plain-language results you can actually understand").
